Fair questions

Do we have to change all our existing tools?+

Not necessarily. We first look at the systems you already use and what they allow us to connect. Any new tools or access requirements are discussed before building.

Does everything happen automatically?+

We decide that together. Sorting, summaries and reminders can be automated where appropriate. Pricing, sensitive messages and important commitments can stay behind a human review step.

What would the first project cost?+

That depends on the process, connections and level of support. The first conversation is about defining a useful scope, not selling a package before understanding the job.
